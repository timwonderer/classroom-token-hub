# Verify a Teacher Recovery Request

## Overview

If your teacher gets locked out of their account, the app does not email them a reset link. It asks their students instead.

When a recovery starts, Classroom Token Hub **randomly selects 2 students in each of your teacher's classes**. If you are one of them, an **Account Recovery Request** message appears on your dashboard. Your teacher does not choose you and is not told who was selected. Helping takes about a minute: you confirm your own passphrase, the app gives you a 6-digit code, and you hand that code to your teacher in person.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Responding to the request

1. Log in to your account. The **Account Recovery Request** banner is at the top of your dashboard, with the date the request expires.
2. Select **Help Verify Identity**.

If you are not ready right now, you can close the banner. It comes back the next time you open your dashboard, until the request is finished or expires.

### Getting a code

1. Read the note explaining why you are being asked.
2. Enter **your own passphrase**, the same one you use to log in.
3. Select **Generate a fresh 30-minute code**.

### Handing over the code

You now see **Verification Successful** and a 6-digit **Recovery Code**.

Give it to your teacher face to face, **only while your teacher is with your class**. The code works for 30 minutes.

## Important notes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Give a code only in person, while your teacher is with your class.** Do not text it, email it, post it, or send it in a chat, and do not give it to anyone else. The reason your teacher asks students instead of a website is that a stranger cannot walk into your classroom.

> [!NOTE]
> **A new code replaces your old one.** If your code expires or gets lost, go back to the request and generate a fresh one. Only your newest code works.

> [!NOTE]
> **One code from your class is enough.** Another student in your class was also selected. Once your teacher has a working code from either of you, your class is done.

> [!NOTE]
> **You are entering your passphrase, not your teacher's.** The app is confirming that you are you. Your passphrase is never shown to your teacher.

> [!NOTE]
> Requests expire after five days. If the expiry date on the banner has passed, the request is over and your teacher needs to start again.
