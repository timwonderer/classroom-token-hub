# Reset or Recover Your Account

## Overview
If you forget your username, PIN or passphrase, or simply cannot log in, your teacher has the ability to securely reset your account so you can regain access.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Recovering Your Account
1. Contact your teacher directly and ask them to "reset" your account.
2. Your teacher will give you a short reset code.
3. On the student sign-in page, select **I can't log into my account**. Enter the reset code and submit.
4. Enter a new word for the **Theme** prompt. The app builds a new username around it, the same way it did when you first set up your account (see [Log In and First-Time Setup](login-setup.md)).
5. Save your new username somewhere you will still have next month. **Classroom Token Hub does not keep a readable copy of it, so your teacher cannot look it up for you.**
6. Select **I've saved my username**, then type it from your saved copy, exactly as written; capital letters count. This step works exactly like first-time setup: pasting is turned off in this box, **Show my username again** gives you another look, and **Can't type in this box?** turns pasting back on if your assistive technology needs it.
7. Once your username matches, create a new **PIN** and a new **Passphrase**, and submit.

Your money, transaction history and classes stay attached to your account. Only your username, PIN and passphrase change.

## Important Notes
> [!TIP]
> **Keep your details safe:** Write down your new username, PIN, and passphrase in a safe, secure location (like a planner or notes app) so you do not have to ask for a reset again.
