# Browse and Buy Items

## Overview

**Store** in your sidebar has two tabs: **Browse Items** and **My Items**. This guide covers browsing and buying. What happens after you buy is in [Track Redemptions](redemption-status.md).

Every item card is covered in badges, and they are not decoration — they tell you what you are actually getting and whether it is worth the money.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Reading an item card

The most important badge is the **item type**, because it decides when you get the thing:

| Badge | What happens when you buy |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediate Use** | You get it right away. Nothing to redeem later. |
| **Delayed Use** | It goes into **My Items**. You redeem it when you are ready, and your teacher approves. |
| **Hall Pass** | It is added to your hall pass balance. |
| **Collective Goal** | Nothing happens until enough classmates buy it too. |

Other badges you may see:

- **Bundle: N items** — one purchase, charged once, that puts N separate copies in **My Items**
- **N% off when you buy N+** — buy that many at once and the whole order is discounted
- **Available: N left** — limited stock
- **Limit: N per student** — a cap on how many you personally may buy

### Rent perks

If your teacher itemized rent, some store items are things your rent already pays for.

- **Included in your rent!** with an **Included in Rent** badge and a faded card means you already have it. The button reads **Already Included** and is disabled — buying it again would be paying twice.
- **Rent Perk** with **N free uses in My Items** or **Unlimited free uses in My Items** means paying rent has already given you those uses. They are sitting in your **My Items** tab, and redeeming one there costs nothing.

The free uses are not a discount on the store price. The card still shows what the item costs, and buying it here is an extra copy at that price — worth doing only once your free uses are gone.

### Collective goals

A collective goal item shows a progress bar reading **N/N** and one of two alerts:

- **Whole Class Goal: All N students must purchase!**
- **Collective Goal: N students needed to unlock**

Below it you get **N more needed!** or **Goal reached! Item will unlock soon.** If your teacher set a deadline, **Deadline: Goal expires [date]** appears too.

Your purchase counts toward the goal immediately. Once the deadline passes the item stops selling — you will be told the goal has closed rather than charged for it. Reaching the target is different: nothing unlocks on its own, and whatever the reward is, your teacher hands it out.

### Buying

Choose **Purchase** to open **Confirm Purchase**. It shows the item, the unit price, and any bundle or discount details.

Set the **quantity** with the `-` and `+` buttons. **Total Price** updates as you change it, and if a bulk discount kicks in you will see **You save $X**. Rent-perk items lock quantity to 1.

**Total Price** is what you are charged. If the screen says **You will get N total uses**, N separate copies arrive in **My Items**, each redeemed on its own — there is no shared counter, so redeeming one leaves the rest untouched.

Then enter your **passphrase** — *Enter your Passphrase to confirm:* — and choose **Confirm Purchase**.

## Important notes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Buying uses your passphrase. Redeeming uses your PIN.** These are two different credentials and the store asks for each in a different place. If the box says *Enter your Passphrase*, your PIN will not work, and the other way round.

> [!WARNING]
> **Reaching a collective goal target does not unlock anything by itself,** even though the screen says *Item will unlock soon.* Your teacher decides what the reward is and when it arrives, so ask them what they intend before you spend. If a goal runs out of time *without* reaching its target, though, you get your money back automatically — the item leaves your inventory and the amount you paid returns to checking, usually within an hour of the deadline.

> [!NOTE]
> **Only your checking account can spend.** Money in savings is not available to the store. Move it to checking first if you need it.

> [!TIP]
> If an item is **Out of Stock** or **Already Included**, the button is disabled — it is not a bug and refreshing will not help. Out of stock may come back; already included means you have it already, so go use it from **My Items** instead.
