### Why this release

- **Insurance before purchase** (#1587): students can read a tiered plan's description before buying it;
  teachers can pick a marketing badge (ported from v1); the policy form shows the Economic Engine's suggested
  premium for the chosen tier and billing frequency.
- **Insurance lifecycle** (#1588, owner ruling 2026-10-14): editing a policy moves its current holders to the new
  version at their next renewal, through a new entitlement at the coverage boundary with no waiting period;
  hiding or retiring a policy ends coverage at the boundary. Migration `f6c8d0e2a4b5` records which version a row
  supersedes.
- **Correctness and hygiene:** three GET routes no longer commit (INV-ARC-007, #1574); support-ticket purchase
  reversal leaves the same record as the banking page's Reverse (#1580); destructive-action timers reject
  non-finite values (#1578); every GitHub Actions reference is pinned to a commit SHA (#1576).

Full detail is in the `[2.2.4]` section of the CHANGELOG.

## Rollback

- **Fix forward** is the default: a reviewed fix on `main`, released the same way.
- **Code only:** release `79cbb2615` through the same workflow. Both new columns are nullable and unread by
  `79cbb2615`, so they can stay in place. Once a teacher edits a policy under v2.2.4, the successor chain and its
  continuation entitlements exist only in v2.2.4's model; rolling back after that leaves the old code renewing the
  superseded version.
- **Restore:** only if data were damaged. Stop the application and restore `cth-db-20261016T035550Z-manual`.
