### Why this release

**The insurance premium suggestion showed monthly rent as less than students are billed (#1592).** During the
v2.2.4 browser checks, `/admin/insurance/new` with a monthly policy read "After rent (**$591.39**)" for a class
whose rent is **$600.00 per month**. `build_insurance_premium_helper` converted rent to a weekly figure and then
rescaled it over the 30-day authoring month. Rent billed at the policy's own frequency is a fixed amount per period,
like the premium, so the helper now shows it as billed and scales only when the frequencies differ. Read-only
guidance on one teacher screen: no write, no money moved. Test
`test_monthly_rent_shows_the_billed_amount_on_a_monthly_policy` fails without the fix with `591.39 == 600.0`.
