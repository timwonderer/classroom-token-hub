### Why this release

After the owner reversed rows 1275 and 1277 under v2.2.1, the student page offered **Reverse** on the $0 "item
removed" entries those reversals wrote. Reversing a purchase writes a refund (`REVERSAL`) and a $0
`void_item_removed` entry in one operation (`SPEC-OPS-001` §3.5); both are the compensation, which is terminal
(§3.1, §3.6). Pressing Reverse on the item-removed entry failed with an error (the database rejects a reversal of
a $0 row), so no money could move, but a teacher was offered an action that can only fail. #1532 names both
types in `REVERSAL_RECORD_TYPES`: the student page offers no Reverse on them, and the reverse action and
`reverse_transaction` refuse them before writing anything.
