### Why this release

The 2026-10-10 security, financial-correctness and operational audit, remediated across #1534, #1536, #1540,
#1541, #1546, #1548, #1549, #1550, #1551, #1552, #1553 and #1554. Highlights:

- **Money:** rent and insurance premiums no longer overdraw checking (F1–F4); a bulk payroll retry can't pay twice
  (F5); overlapping rent reconciliation can't double-assess (F8, migration below); hall-pass reservation and
  "max students out" races closed (F6, F7); roster balance figures and earnings corrected (F9); a bad transfer
  account name no longer returns a 500 (F10).
- **Security:** Turnstile required in production and verified on the sysadmin login (S3, S5); per-account login
  budgets and TOTP replay protection (S5); forwarded-IP headers trusted only from the proxy (S7); server-side PIN
  format (S8); cryptographic username RNG (S9); no decrypted names in the session cookie (S10).
- **Load:** per-seat tap budgets and a narrower shared login budget (O1); hourly jobs on distinct cron minutes and
  no full attendance scan (O2, O3); per-request attendance memoization (O4); scheduler hand-off waits for running
  jobs (O5); a capped 404 cache (O8).

Full detail is in the `[2.2.3]` section of the CHANGELOG.
