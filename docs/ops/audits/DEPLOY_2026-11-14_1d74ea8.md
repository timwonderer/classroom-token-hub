# Release record: v2.4.5, `1d74ea8`

Released 2026-11-14 at 07:14 UTC (Friday 2026-11-13, 23:14 PST), outside school hours.

**Type:** Tagged release under `SOP-DEP-002` §VII.A, **no migration**. Its security fixes:
- Transaction insurance no longer accepts rent, late fees or other non-insurable charges as a claim.
- Signed-in student routes are rate limited per seat, not per shared school address (#1642).
- Hall-pass adjustment errors no longer expose exception text.

It also removes:
- the leftover NSF/overdraft-fee machinery (#1647);
- the unused OpenTelemetry packages (#1753);
- the Store's Delete Permanently button, which the route always refused (#1652).

Other changes are four help-link fixes, a payroll-cycle record fix binding the Economic Engine to the cycle's
boundary, and SOP and document corrections. Full detail is in the `[2.4.5]` section of the CHANGELOG.
