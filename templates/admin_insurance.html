{% endcall %} {% call help.topic('ins-states', 'visibility', 'For sale, hidden, and retired') %}

The three availability states are not the same thing:

  • For sale — students can buy it now.
  • Hidden — no new purchases, but everyone already covered stays covered until their term ends. Reversible with Put back on sale.
  • Retired — permanent. The terms can never be offered again.
{% endcall %} {% call help.topic('ins-edit', 'edit_note', 'Editing a policy someone already bought') %}

Editing a policy does not change anyone's coverage. A purchase freezes the terms as they stood that day, so your edits apply to future buyers only. If you need current holders on new terms, hide the old policy and publish a replacement.
