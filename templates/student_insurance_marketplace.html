{% extends "layout_student.html" %} {% set page_title = "Insurance" %} {% set current_page = "insurance" %} {% import "macros/cards.html" as cards %} {% block title %}Insurance{% endblock %} {% block page_icon %}shield{% endblock %} {% block contextual_help_body %} {{ help.panel_lead("Policies you can buy, the coverage you already hold, and the claims you have filed against it.") }} {% call help.topic('ins-what', 'shield', 'What a policy actually buys') %}

You pay a premium on a schedule. In return, if the thing the policy covers happens to you, you can file a claim and be paid back for part or all of it.

If nothing happens, you do not get the premiums back. That is not a bug — it is what you were buying. Insurance is worth it when the loss you are protecting against would hurt more than the premiums do.

{% endcall %} {% call help.topic('ins-before', 'fact_check', 'Read these before you buy') %}
  • Waiting period — how long after buying before coverage starts. Buying a policy after the thing has already happened does not cover it.
  • Claim limits — the most one claim can pay, and how many claims you get in a period.
  • Premium and frequency — how much leaves checking, and how often. This keeps charging you.
{% endcall %} {% call help.topic('ins-paying', 'payments', 'Keep the premium paid') %}

Each premium is billed a few days before its period starts and taken from checking right away. If checking is short, the premium stays unpaid and your coverage pauses when the new period starts, so claims are refused until you pay it on the policy page. Depending on the policy, unpaid premiums either keep adding up or end your coverage after a set number of days. Some policies also make you wait before you can buy them again.

{% endcall %} {% call help.topic('ins-claims', 'assignment_turned_in', 'Filing and tracking claims') %}

File from the policy itself, within its claim deadline, and say plainly what happened. Your claims shows where each one stands. Approval is your teacher's decision, and a claim can be capped or refused if it is outside the terms you bought.
