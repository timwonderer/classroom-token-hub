Genesis
The Original Problem & Design Decisions
Pre-2024 → November 2025
Philosophy Architecture
expand_more

The project began as a full-time high school teacher's answer to a gap in educational technology: no platform simulated classroom economies realistically while respecting student data privacy.

"I will just make it myself."

Five Core Design Decisions Crystallized:
  • Experience Before Explanation — Students understand systems after feeling them, not from lectures
  • Join Code as Source of Truth — Early designs tied students to teacher accounts and failed in multi-teacher classrooms; join code became the absolute source of class isolation, reshaping the entire data model
  • Teacher Authority with Transparency — No invisible overrides; all teacher actions visible to students
  • Classroom Wage Index (CWI) — Macro-level stabilizer to prevent runaway wages and economic collapse
  • Insurance as Reality Response — Attendance isn't purely a moral choice; punishment disguised as realism is neither accurate nor educational
The Name Change: "Classroom Economy" → "Classroom Token Hub"

Not just a rebrand — a philosophical shift from a gimmick to a persistent, interconnected system. A hub for comprehensive financial literacy.

Key Learning: Real students find real bugs that simulated testing misses. Security must be foundational, not retrofitted.

P0 Crisis
Same-Teacher Multi-Period Data Leak
November 2025
Critical Fix Security
expand_more
Impact: Students in multiple class periods with the same teacher saw aggregated financial data across all periods — a fundamental privacy and fairness violation.

Root Cause:
  • Transaction model had teacher_id but not join_code
  • All queries filtered by teacher_id only, returning data across all periods
Resolution:
  • Added join_code to all financial tables
  • Updated session management to track current_join_code
  • Modified ALL queries to scope by join_code
  • Created interactive backfill for legacy transactions
  • Added comprehensive multi-tenancy regression tests
Lesson: Multi-tenancy must be enforced at the database level, not the application level. join_code is non-negotiable.

Crisis
The Great Decimal Precision Crisis
January 2026
Critical Fix
expand_more
Symptoms: Zero-balance transfers triggering $35 overdraft fees • Partial rent payments leaving unpayable tiny balances ($0.0000001) • Dashboard interest calculations crashing with TypeErrors

Root Cause:
  • Transaction.amount stored as Float instead of Numeric
  • IEEE 754 floating-point: 0.1 + 0.2 ≠ 0.3
  • Mixed Decimal/float arithmetic caused TypeErrors
Lesson: Financial systems MUST use Decimal type, never Float. Precision errors compound invisibly and become hidden bugs.

lightbulb No, we are not about that life

Classroom Token Hub was created as a classroom economy system that fosters responsibility and shared accountability. Flashy features and gamification are antithesis to our design principles. The following features were intentially omitted to preserve the integrity and consistency with our philosophy.

NO XP BARS OR STREAKS Earned wages and time-based pay. We reward consistency through tangible outcomes that students can understand. What we build: Real wages derived from time with real consequences
NO PERFECT ATTENDANCE BONUSES Realign attendance and productivity as baseline expectations while insurance protects against unexpected deviation from baseline. What we build: Insurance system handles legitimate absences and personal lapses in responsibilities
NO INVISIBLE TEACHER OVERRIDES Mysterious teacher action erodes trust in the system and trivializes consequences as "as teacher wishes" What we build: All actions logged and visible to students
NO BEHAVIOR SCORES No engagement ratings or compliance metrics What we build: Neutral logs supporting teacher conversations
NO AI JUDGMENTS OF INTENT No motivation predictions or effort ratings What we build: Neutral logs that don't infer character
NO UNLIMITED CONFIG KNOBS Arbitrary overrides break system coherence What we build : Guardrailed settings that preserve integrity
NO FRICTIONLESS SHORTCUTS When process slows down and forces cognitive engagement, mistakes become rarity What we build: Deliberate processes requiring acknowledgment
NO LEADERBOARDS Public rankings don't belong in classrooms What we build: Private balances protect student dignity
NO NEUTRALITY CLAIM All systems have assumptions, visible assumptions allow for discussions and adjustments while hidden assumptions breed resentment and distrust when they inevitably surface. What we build: Visible assumptions open to class discussion
NO UNSUPERVISED STUDENT ACCESS Teachers should always be the first person to resolve student issues. What we build: Student support tickets are triaged by teacher first, always.

In reflection

The system you are looking at today is conceptualized, designed, built, tested, maintained, and updated by a full-time high school teacher who lives by the motto “I will just make it myself.”

Becoming an app developer was never part of the plan, but rather a side project that has “gone out of hand.” Every feature and design you see today was a product of countless 500 errors, tears, students’ incessant “Mr., it’s not working!”, and, of course, the satisfaction of seeing the puzzle pieces click into place.

This project would not have been possible without the best beta testers in the world: real students. Every impatient double click, negative deposit, “what if I do this instead?”, and middle-of-the-night transaction exposed bugs and vulnerabilities that would have remained silent for now, but would fail loudly in production.

Classroom Token Hub was created to fill the void of a platform that simulates an economy more realistically than what is available on the market. It also functions as a classroom management tool that teaches students personal responsibility. Most of all, it respects data privacy and security like no other. The friction you experience when creating, claiming, and resetting accounts was intentional. Classroom Token Hub does not ask, “How can we defend ourselves?” It was designed to anticipate, “What if the bad actor gets in?”

Welcome to the Classroom Economy System, reimagined.
