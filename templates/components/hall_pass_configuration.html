How It Works

  • Students Out At Once: Maximum number of students who may be out for this destination; 0 blocks the destination.
  • Total Students Out At Once: Read-only sum of destination limits. Approvals are blocked when the number currently out reaches the smaller of this sum and the class-wide Out Limit above the tabs.
