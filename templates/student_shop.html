Use Item sends a redemption request to your teacher with whatever details you provide, and is confirmed with your PIN. Say clearly what you are asking for — a vague request is slower to approve.

The request has to be accepted before anything takes effect. Send it before you need it, not at the moment you need it.

Spending money asks for your passphrase; redeeming something you already own asks for your PIN. Both exist so nobody else on a shared or unlocked device can spend or redeem in your name. Do not let someone else type either one.
