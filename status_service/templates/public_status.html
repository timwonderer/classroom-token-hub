What the numbers mean

How service estimates and measurements work

Service cards are estimates from recent requests: Yes means observations are within thresholds; Probably not means elevated errors or latency; Possibly down means at least ten requests with 50% or more HTTP 5xx. Missing or limited evidence remains Not recently verified. These estimates do not test every classroom action.

Elevated server-error responses means more than 2% returned HTTP 5xx. Elevated not-found responses means more than 5% returned HTTP 404; an expected missing resource can also cause a 404. High latency means p95 exceeds 1,500 ms. Crossings are shown even with limited traffic; within expected range requires at least 20 requests.

p80 and p95 are the response times below which 80% and 95% of measured requests fall. Application requests include health polling. Route groups cover selected endpoints; classroom economy does not represent every economic operation.

History shows eligible five-minute windows within thresholds, sampled once per minute. Windows overlap. It is not uptime or request success. Low/no traffic and missing or stale monitoring are excluded. Coverage compares eligible measured windows with scheduled minutes. Dates use UTC.
