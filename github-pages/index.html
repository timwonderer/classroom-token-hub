Thank you for your patience

Now counting down to v2.0

We have redesigned your classroom economy system, reimagined.

On July 1, 2026, Classroom Token Hub v1.10 was officially retired after a short demonstration run that lasted for 7 months. During those 7 months, our own students extensively poked at our architecture with the persistence and creativity rivaling that of professional penetration testers. We learned a lot about our system and ourselves during that time

After the retirement of v1.10, we began a complete redesign and rewrite of Classroom Token Hub. Almost all legacy code structure were explicitly retired and replaced with newer, invariant-driven architecture.

The result is a classroom economy platform that is truly one of a kind with a real economic engine and strict commitment to the correctness and visibility of all transactions. We have built a platform that we would want to use with our own students and we hope you will feel the same.

We are committed to bring you software we would use with our students

As a classroom teacher, software that is good enough is not enough to ship when other teachers and students depend on it. Especially when the developer is also an end user. Suddenly, an error screen on a Monday morning before first period starts is no longer a test case. It is an emergency.

For returning users, welcome back! When you open up v2.0 for the first time, you will notice some minor changes to the interface. However, you will find these changes to improve your experiences in a subtle yet profound way.

If this is your first time here, welcome! We designed Classroom Token Hub to be intuitive, straightforward, self-explanatory so you and your students can focus on teaching and learning.
