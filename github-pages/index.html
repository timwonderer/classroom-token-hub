Classroom Token Hub v2.0

Preparing for launch

A new version for your classroom

Classroom Token Hub was originally developed in 2024 as a personal project to manage a classroom economy. While other platforms exist, we could not find one that offered all the features we needed along with the transparency we wanted for our students. We also found that many platforms designed their interfaces to be either too childish or too sterile.

We started with a simple question: How can we design a classroom economy system that respects teacher agency, follows clear and explicit rules, provides meaningful guidance, and protects user privacy?

After almost three months of architectural rewrite, we have worked toward that goal with Classroom Token Hub v2.0.

What's happening now?

Version 2.0 is in prelaunch testing and review. Here's what you should know about v2.0 launch.

  • New System, New Account

    Version 2 uses a new account and classroom model. Returning users will need to create a new account and set up their classes when v2 launches.

  • New Support Email Address

    During v1, the only way to report issues is through the in app ticket system after sign in. Now, you may report issues that occurred before signing in to support@classroomtokenhub.com

  • New Status Page

    The status page is designed to report feature availability and incident updates. Check it when you need information about service interruptions.

Stay tuned, we are almost done.

We always knew being a solo developer while working full time as a teacher is challenging. For the past two months, we encountered many setbacks as well discovery layers upon layers of scaffolds that miraculously held v1.10 up. It was then we realized just how lucky we are to come so close to catastrophe yet never actually experience it. That's why we have chosen to rewrite the whole app.

The rewrite was immensely difficult and there are times when the easier option was to patch the issue with a compatibility bridge and move on. However, our stubborness pushed us to establish strict development practices and review processes to make sure we are not operating on will power alone. As a classroom teacher, software that is good enough is not enough to ship when other teachers and students depend on it. Especially when the developer is also an end user. Suddenly, an error screen on a Monday morning before first period starts is no longer a test case. It is an emergency.

For returning users, welcome back! When you open up v2.0 for the first time, you will notice some minor changes to the interface. However, you will find these changes to improve your experiences in a subtle yet profound way.

If this is your first time here, welcome! We designed Classroom Token Hub to be intuitive, straightforward, self-explanatory so you and your students can focus on teaching and learning.

We cannot wait to share v2.0 with you. We built Classroom Token Hub to be your classroom economy system, reimagined.
