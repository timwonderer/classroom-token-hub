2. What Is Classroom Token Hub?

Classroom Token Hub is an educational web application designed to help students learn about financial literacy through a simulated classroom economy. Teachers can use it to manage virtual currency, track student participation, and create engaging lessons about money management.

3. Account Responsibilities

A class’s records belong to the teacher who provisioned it. Roster notes are visible to that teacher alone; display names appear only in the teacher’s roster and the student’s own interface. Both are encrypted in storage. A support report may be escalated to support staff, who receive only what the teacher ticks category by category — balances, the reported transaction, recent transactions, the student’s own report text, and the class name — alongside technical diagnostics. Display names and roster notes are in no part of that disclosure. See the Privacy & Data Handling policy for access and encryption details.

Students sign in with a username and passphrase; a PIN is used for selected classroom actions. Teachers sign in with a username and authenticator code (TOTP), or an enrolled passkey. Credentials belong to one account holder and are not shared.

Students can ask their teacher for a recovery code. Entering that code starts a time-limited reset session; no name or join code is required. Teacher recovery instead requires student confirmation from every owned class. See account recovery details.

Students and teachers protect their accounts by:

  • Keeping credentials private: sign-in credentials are not shared. A student hands a teacher-recovery confirmation code to their own teacher, in person, and only during a requested recovery.
  • Accounting for their own activity: whatever happens on an account is attributed to its holder, which is what makes keeping sign-in details secure worth the trouble.
  • Reporting security problems at once: a student who suspects someone else has reached their account tells their teacher; a teacher raises it through support.

6. Account Termination

Teachers can delete their accounts or classes. Deleting the last student in a class also deletes that class; deleting the teacher’s final class deletes the teacher account. Inactive teacher accounts are deleted after 180 days without sign-in, or 30 days after creation if never signed in. These application deletions have no undelete option. Unclaim is a separate action that preserves the class participation record. See retention and deletion details.

In very rare cases, we may need to terminate any accounts that could disrupt the normal operation of Classroom Token Hub. These actions may be taken without prior notice and are irreversible. Here are some potential reasons that could result in account termination:

  • Violate these Terms of Service
